Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 August 2026

This policy explains what information McNeil Development, LLC (“McNeil Development,” “we,” “us”) collects through mcneil-development.com, why we collect it, and how we handle it.

Information we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us. If you submit the seller inquiry form, we receive the details you enter:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number, if you provide one
  • The property address you are asking about
  • Any message you write

We do not run advertising trackers, analytics profiling, or third-party marketing pixels on this site, and we do not use cookies to identify you.

How we use it

We use what you send solely to respond to your inquiry and to discuss a possible transaction involving your property. We do not sell, rent, or trade your information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

We may share details with people who need them to evaluate or complete a transaction you have asked us about — for example attorneys, lenders, or title companies — and where we are required to by law.

Service providers

This site is hosted by Netlify, which processes and stores form submissions on our behalf and records standard server logs such as IP address and browser type for security and reliability. Web fonts are served by Google Fonts, which receives the request needed to deliver the font files. These providers act as processors for us and are not permitted to use your information for their own purposes.

How long we keep it

We retain inquiry submissions for as long as needed to respond and to maintain records of any resulting transaction. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the information you have submitted, or ask us to stop contacting you. Email ryan.mcneil51@gmail.com and we will act on the request.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS, and form submissions are transmitted encrypted. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect what you send us.

Children

This site is intended for adults conducting real estate business. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the revised version here and update the date above.
