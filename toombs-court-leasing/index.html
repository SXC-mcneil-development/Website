Neighborhood & News

Life around Toombs Court.

21 Old South Road — gourmet market with fresh seafood, prime meats, prepared foods, and a café, minutes from Toombs Court.

Nantucket Health Club

Mid-island fitness facility offering cardio, weights, and group classes including Pilates, TRX, and yoga.

Toombs Court sits directly on the Old South Road Bike Path, linking the property to town and the south shore.

Getting Around

Catch the wave.

Toombs Court residents enjoy access to the NRTA shuttle — Nantucket's free, island-wide transit, with eleven routes connecting every beach, village, and ferry. The dedicated Mid Island Route 08 runs directly to and from downtown, with easy connections to the airport and Fast Ferry.
