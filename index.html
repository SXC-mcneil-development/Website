Who We Are

McNeil Development builds with the craft, character, and permanence that coastal New England deserves.

Founded in 2024, McNeil Development has rapidly grown into a portfolio of more than $100M in assets under management — anchored by ground-up developments on the island of Nantucket.

From new construction to full gut renovations and rehabilitations, across both single-family and multifamily projects, we manage every phase in-house: acquisition, design, construction, and final sale. The result is homes built with meticulous attention to detail and a deep respect for the places they belong to.
